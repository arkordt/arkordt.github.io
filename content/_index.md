I am a PhD candidate at the [Institute for Theoretical Astrophysics at the Centre for Astronomy of Heidelberg University](https://zah.uni-heidelberg.de).
My research aims to understand the formation, properties, and feedback of Population III (Pop III) stars in controlled numerical experiments and in the formation and evolution of young galaxies of the early universe. 
The central question is what regulates metal enrichment across stellar generations and how the chemical imprints of the first stars are preserved and observable in later populations.

My primary tool is high-resolution hydrodynamic cosmological simulation with the AREPO code, augmented with non-equilibrium chemistry and radiative transfer. 
In controlled numerical experiments, I investigate which the metal signatures of Pop III supernovae are imprinted in second-generation stars.
I am involved the analysis of a cosmological zoom-in simulation suite, which resolves individual stars in low- to medium-mass galaxies until the Epoch of Reionization to find out when, where and until which time Pop III stars form and if what this means for current and planend observations with JWST or ELT.
